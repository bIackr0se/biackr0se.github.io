New posts land in the RSS feed. Point any feed reader (NetNewsWire, Feedly, Miniflux, Thunderbird) at:

```
https://biackr0se.github.io/blog/index.xml
```

Most readers also find it automatically if you just give them `biackr0se.github.io/blog`.

If you clicked expecting a page and got a wall of XML somewhere, that wall was the feed itself. It is meant for machines; this page is for you.
